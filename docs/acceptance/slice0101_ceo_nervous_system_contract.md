# Slice 010.1 — CEO Nervous System Contract

This frozen contract completes the bounded Company operating loop.

## Memory

CEO calls receive bounded deterministic working, episodic, Brain, Meeting,
Operation, Project, and workforce context. Persisted summaries are preferred
over raw transcripts and audit history. Retrieval makes no provider call.

## Automatic internal execution

One Founder operation approval authorizes internal Task, Review, CEO decision,
Meeting, HR-assessment, and final-report calls while Company, Operation, and
applicable Meeting budgets permit. Each browser step performs at most one
provider operation. Actual cost is reconciled and reduces remaining authority.
No automatic retry follows paid or ambiguous failure.

## Completion and decision

One central completion guard forbids completion while Tasks are blocked,
Reviews are unfinished, Founder decisions are unresolved, required Meetings
are unresolved, or completion criteria remain unsatisfied. Deterministic
routing handles obvious work. Genuine conflict, blockage, ambiguity, or
capability gaps invoke a strict CEO decision step.

## Debate

The CEO decision may choose a bounded Meeting according to the approved
meeting policy. It reuses the existing Meeting engine, chooses involved
Employees, advances through browser-owned steps, and returns persisted Meeting
results to the Operation without Founder transfer or start actions.

## Workforce

Founder, CEO, and Directors can submit governed staffing needs. The HR Director
performs the assessment as one auditable provider execution. Application code
calculates financial exposure. Founder reviews HR's recommendation and remains
the only hiring authority. Approved Department, Position, manager, ModelConfig,
resource envelope, and probation target are persisted and used exactly.

## Paid-call safety

Operation steps persist a logical identity and the provider-call boundary.
Stale or ambiguous provider-started work never calls again automatically.
Known successful AgentRuns are materialized instead of repurchased when safe.
Unknown payment truth transitions the Operation to Founder attention.

## Safety

There is no background worker, automatic budget expansion, automatic paid
retry, automatic employee scoring, vector search, web search, external action,
or fabricated Talent library.
