# Eason One V0.12.1 — Founder Delegation + CEO Learning Foundation

This is a delta update on top of V0.12.0 hotfix2. It does not replace the repository, `.venv`, `.env`, `.git`, `instance`, People, Meetings, or existing History.

## Founder contract

- Founder gives a normal outcome-oriented request instead of a technical orchestration prompt.
- Normal delegated work enters `AUTO_DELEGATION`: the persistent CEO chooses the smallest sufficient team and workflow from current capability, relevant History, cost, risk, and the active CEO policy.
- Founder approves the Mission boundary. A Founder-stated TWD ceiling is parsed deterministically and is a hard cap; unrelated numbers such as provider-call limits are not treated as money.
- The model's `budget_twd` is retained for audit but never becomes authority by itself.
- If no Founder ceiling is stated, the Governor derives a priced envelope from the complete workflow plus bounded contingency.
- Every paid stage has its own hard cap, every call has a hard cap, and verification/final-delivery authority is reserved before discretionary work.

## CEO learning

- CEO is a persistent Employee, not a frozen router and not the underlying model.
- Relevant prior real-Mission episodes are retrieved with provenance; unrelated recent Missions are not injected merely because they are recent.
- Terminal real Missions create an outcome-linked Research Ledger episode containing route, tasks, employees, meetings, calls, costs, Founder actions, contribution events, report, and terminal result.
- The system also creates an unvalidated CEO policy-learning proposal in the CEO dossier.
- Only Founder-validated learning is injected into the active CEO policy. No silent self-modification is allowed.

## Research findings implemented in runtime

- **Debate only when needed:** `ON_MATERIAL_CONFLICT` Meetings are evaluated after prerequisite work.
- **Adaptive stop:** if persisted results show no material conflict, the planned Meeting is closed at zero provider cost with an explicit event and minutes.
- **Sparse/delta communication:** the existing bounded Meeting packet and structured Claim/Challenge/Evidence protocol remain authoritative; full transcripts are not re-injected into every call.
- **Artifact/outcome over process completion:** completion reserve protects Goal Verification and final Founder delivery.
- **Durable/idempotent execution:** the checkpoint, lease, event, and pre-call reservation kernel remains authoritative.
- **Outcome-linked History:** CEO learning is based on real execution and outcomes, not self-reported confidence.

## Organization rules

- Reviewers are optional, never inserted merely because a manager exists.
- Research Director is not a default review tax.
- Meetings remain first-class visible rooms and People/Meetings remain primary Headquarters navigation.
- Repository work still routes CEO → Engineer → local Codex → diff/tests → Engineer acceptance.

## Verification fix

`verify-v0121.ps1` uses a dedicated writable temp directory under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\EasonOne` and disables the broken global/project pytest cache. It avoids the previous `pytest-of-eason` and `.pytest_cache` permission failures.

## Explicit non-goals

This update does not claim to finish public Store/Marketplace, Eason Market, automatic promotion/salary, reliable contribution scoring, voice, or enterprise distributed execution. It installs the governed learning/history foundation required before those systems can be trustworthy.
