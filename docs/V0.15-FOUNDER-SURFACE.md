# Eason One v0.15 — Founder Surface Pilot

This is a product-surface correction over the installed v0.14.1 backbone.

- Keeps Eason One's existing dark/cyan brand language.
- Does not translate the product into Chinese.
- Keeps the existing People and Meeting Room experiences.
- Removes the mistaken `/home` product shell by redirecting it back to Headquarters.
- Adds a dedicated CEO Office without making CEO the entire product.
- Makes Home quiet: Active Projects, Needs Founder, live People/Meetings, CEO direct line.
- Makes Projects list-like rather than dashboard-like.
- Makes Project Detail a workspace/document with Work Items, Evidence, Decisions, Meetings, Company Truth.
- Adds History as the append-only CompanyEvent observation surface.
- Keeps Mission Control, Memory, Finance and System available under collapsed Advanced navigation.

This pilot intentionally does not attempt the full Economy / Skill / Market UI yet.
